Before you pick a plan

Do students need to download anything?

No. Games run in the browser on laptops, phones and tablets.

Will it match the method book I use?

Yes. The curriculum matches all major method books, so games reinforce what you're already teaching in class.

How do students sign in?

Band Essentials integrates with Clever and ClassLink, so students can get in the same way they reach their other class tools.

How much work is it for me?

None beyond assigning games. There's no prep, and the progress dashboard shows accuracy, completion, speed and growth automatically.

Which plan should I choose?

Individual students pick Theta Player ($9/month or $39/year). Private instructors pick Theta Studio (1 teacher, 30 students). Band directors pick Theta School (500 students, 5 teachers).

Can my school pay by purchase order?

Yes. Purchase orders, PayPal, and all major credit cards are accepted, and you can request a W-9 from the pricing page.
